Accounting & administrative systems setup

“I want my books in order and my work organised.”

Books kept in order from source document to report, with a clear system and procedures that cut errors and reliance on memory.

  • A chart of accounts and a documented document cycle
  • Corrected records and clear opening balances
  • An accounting system (software or Excel) with training
  • A written manual of procedures and controls

Financial management & periodic follow-up

“I want to know my real profit, control cash and track performance.”

Regular reports and closings, profitability and cash analysis, and a budget to compare actual results with the plan.

  • Financial statements and a monthly management report
  • Profitability and cost analysis by product

FAQ

Quick answers before you contact us

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. We offer a free first session of up to 45 minutes for owners of running or about-to-launch businesses, after you answer a short questionnaire that helps us understand your needs.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the scope of work and the size of the business. Once we understand your needs, we send you a written proposal with the scope, timeline and fees, as a monthly plan or for a specific service.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

We serve businesses across Palestine, on-site or remotely, depending on the service and what we agree with the client.

How do you keep my data confidential?

We use your data only to carry out the agreed work and never share it with a third party without your consent or unless required by law. We never ask for passwords or bank details through the website forms.

Do you provide statutory audits or tax returns?

Our services are accounting, financial and advisory services and do not include issuing certified statutory audit reports. If you need a service outside our scope, we tell you so from the start.

What do I need to prepare before the first session?

It's enough to know the challenge you want to solve. If you have bank statements, Excel files or ledgers, they help us understand your situation faster.

Want to know your business's real financial position?

Tell us what you need and we'll get back to you within one business day.
