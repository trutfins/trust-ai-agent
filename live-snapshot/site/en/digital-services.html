Financial & credit analysis

Available now

Multi-year financial statement analysis, with an optional credit bureau (PMA) report for credit officers, ending in a score, an advisory recommendation and a report. Note: the English interface is partial; some screens and the report are still in Arabic.

  • Inputs: Company details and 2–5 years of statements (Excel, Word, PDF, images or manual entry); the bureau report is optional, for credit officers.
  • Outputs: Ratios, cash flow, a score against the policy, an advisory recommendation, and a PDF/Excel report.
  • Access: A TRUST account approved by TRUST; activation within 24 working hours of payment confirmation.
  • Cost: Try it free: one analysis showing the results and dashboard. Included in the Credit & Institutions plan ($59 a month), with the Smart Financial Agent, PMA inquiry, the final report and exports.

Smart CFO

Available now

Upload your trial balance to start preparing your financial statements and analysing performance. Add budget and prior-period data to compare results and build forecasts, with commentary explaining the findings and their limitations.

  • Inputs: Trial balance (required); budget, prior periods and receivable/payable ageing (optional). Excel or CSV up to 25 MB.
  • Outputs: Financial statements, derived cash flow, budget and prior-year comparisons, trends, working capital, and a report.
  • Access: The same TRUST account, approved by TRUST; activation within 24 working hours of payment confirmation.
  • Cost: Try it free: your statements and comparisons on screen. Included in the unified plans from Basic ($15 a month) and above, with the Smart Financial Agent, the final report and exports.

Invoice-to-Journal

Available now

Upload an invoice image or PDF; its data is extracted and a journal entry is proposed against your company's chart of accounts. Nothing is posted automatically: another user reviews and approves each entry before it is posted.

  • Inputs: Purchase and sales invoices as images (JPG, PNG or WEBP) or PDF up to 20 MB and 8 pages, plus the company's chart of accounts, tax settings and exchange rates.
  • Outputs: Reviewed invoice data, a draft journal entry for approval and posting, and an audit log of every step, with multi-company and role support.
  • Access: The same TRUST account, approved by TRUST for this tool; activation within 24 working hours of payment confirmation.
  • Cost: Included in the unified plans from Professional ($29 a month) and above, with the Smart Financial Agent.
