Clarity in Numbers, Confidence in Decisions

Introduction

TRUST provides accounting, financial and advisory services for small businesses, medium-sized enterprises and entrepreneurs across Palestine. We help them organise their accounts, understand their financial position and make better decisions about profitability, cash flow, costs and growth.

Who is behind TRUST?

Luai Antari لؤي عنتري

Founder of TRUST · Financial and accounting consultant

TRUST was founded and is led by Luai Antari, a financial and accounting consultant who has worked in the banking and financial sector since 2007. He holds a master's degree in finance, with a thesis on financial performance and governance, and has completed a number of specialised programmes and courses, including the Certified Management Accountant (CMA) programme and risk-based internal auditing.

Over his career he has gained hands-on experience in branch management, credit, internal audit, financial analysis and reporting, and he trains finance professionals in financial and credit analysis.

He personally takes care of understanding each client's needs, defining the scope of work, following up on delivery and explaining the results in practical terms that business owners can use in their day-to-day decisions.

Why was TRUST founded?

We believe that many businesses do not suffer from a lack of effort, but from a lack of clarity in their numbers. Sales can rise while cash falls, or a business can look profitable without the profit ever showing up as cash. That is why we start by organising the data, then preparing the reports, and then analysing what the numbers mean for management's decisions.

What do we offer?

We help you set up accounting and administrative systems, prepare reports and follow up on your finances, analyse profitability, costs and cash flow, prepare budgets and forecasts, and build feasibility studies and financial models for growth and financing decisions.

Our aim is not just to hand over a file, but to help you understand what is happening in your business and know the next practical step.

How do we work?

We start with a session to understand your business and the challenge you face. We then review the available data and set out the scope of work, deliverables, timeline and fees in a written proposal. We carry out the work, deliver the results with an explanation session, and then follow up according to the nature of the service and what we agreed.

Our commitments

  • A clear scope of work, deliverables and fees before we start.
  • Numbers explained in practical language, without unnecessary complexity.
  • Every deliverable reviewed before it is handed over.
  • Client data kept confidential.
  • Clarity on what is and is not included in the service.
  • No promises of results we do not control.

Who do we work with?

We serve small businesses, medium-sized enterprises and entrepreneurs across Palestine, including restaurants and cafés, shops, workshops and small factories, service companies, contractors, and home-based and online businesses.
