Our services

Three core services covering the needs of small and medium businesses in Palestine, from putting the books in order to growth and financing decisions.

Any sub-service can be requested on its own, and the scope and fees are agreed before work starts. You are not tied to a package or a monthly subscription.
Service 1

Accounting & administrative systems setup

“I want my books in order and my work organised.”

We put your accounts in order from the ground up and give you a system and procedures your team works with every day, so you start from reliable numbers.

Who it suits

  • A business recording in ledgers or scattered files
  • A new business that wants to start with the right system
  • A business that has grown, hired more staff and now needs controls

Key deliverables

  • A chart of accounts and a documented document cycle
  • Corrected records and clear opening balances
  • An accounting system (software or Excel) with training
  • A written manual of procedures and controls
Sub-services (7)
  • Setting up and organising the accounting cycle
  • Organising accounts and reviewing records
  • Designing accounting systems and forms
  • Improving procedures and internal controls
  • Designing forms and workflows
  • Excel-based accounting systems
  • Automating recurring finance tasks

Service 2

Financial management & periodic follow-up

“I want to know my real profit, control cash and track performance.”

We turn your numbers into regular reports and indicators so you know your real profit and manage cash before it surprises you.

Who it suits

  • A running business that wants regular monthly follow-up
  • An owner whose sales are good but cash is tight
  • Management that needs short reports for decision-making

Key deliverables

  • Financial statements and a monthly management report
  • Profitability and cost analysis by product
  • Cash flow forecast and a liquidity plan
  • A budget, KPIs and a tracking dashboard
Sub-services (13)
  • Preparing financial statements
  • Periodic reconciliations and closings
  • Financial analysis and ratio interpretation
  • Preparing budgets
  • Cash flow and financial planning
  • Cost and profitability analysis
  • Management reports and KPI dashboards
  • Developing management reports
  • Improving operational efficiency
  • Defining key performance indicators
  • Budget templates
  • Interactive reports and dashboards
  • Data analysis models and periodic reports

Service 3

Feasibility studies & growth and financing decisions

“I want to assess a project or expansion and decide with confidence.”

We test your idea or expansion with numbers before you commit money, and prepare the financial information the decision needs.

Who it suits

  • An entrepreneur with an idea for a new business
  • A running business considering a branch, product or new market
  • An owner facing a big decision: equipment, hiring, pricing

Key deliverables

  • A feasibility study with financial projections
  • A business plan with a financial annex
  • A financial model and break-even analysis
  • Scenarios and a financing needs analysis
Sub-services (7)
  • Economic and financial feasibility studies
  • Preparing business plans
  • Building financial models
  • Break-even analysis
  • Assessing investment scenarios
  • Growth and expansion plans
  • Financial and operational decision support

Scope of our services

Our services are accounting, financial and advisory services. They do not include issuing certified statutory audit reports or representing clients before official bodies. Excel and automation are tools we use to deliver the services, not a separate service line. The scope, deliverables, timeline and fees of each service are set in a written proposal once we understand your needs, and final decisions remain the responsibility of the business's management.
