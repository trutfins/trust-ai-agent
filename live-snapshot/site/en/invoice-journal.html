Invoice-to-Journal

Upload an invoice image or PDF; its data is extracted and a journal entry is proposed against your company's chart of accounts. Nothing is posted automatically: another user reviews and approves each entry before it is posted.

Request accessUser sign-in

Who is it for?

  • Accountants who key in purchase and sales invoices by hand and want to cut data-entry time.
  • CFOs and accounting firms that want entry separated from approval, with every entry reviewed before posting.

What you need to provide

Required

  • The invoice: a clear image or a PDF.
  • A company set up in the tool with its chart of accounts.

Optional

  • Tax settings (configured for your company; no tax treatment is imposed by default).
  • Dated exchange rates entered by your company for invoices in other currencies (no live FX feed).
  • Other users with different roles: upload, post, admin.

File types and limits: JPG, PNG and WEBP images and PDF files, up to 20 MB per file and 8 pages per PDF.

What you get

  • Invoice data extracted by AI, shown next to the original for review and correction.
  • A draft journal entry against your chart of accounts.
  • An approval path: draft → pending review → approved and posted, or rejected.
  • An audit log of who uploaded, reviewed and approved, and when.
  • A dashboard of documents at each stage, per company.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your TRUST account, request access inside the tool and wait for TRUST approval.
  2. Add your company and its chart of accounts, and set tax and exchange rates if needed.
  3. In “Upload”, upload the invoice and request extraction.
  4. Review the extracted data and the draft entry, correct what is needed, then submit it for review.
  5. Another user with posting rights approves and posts the entry, or rejects it.

Account and access

The tool uses the same TRUST account as “Financial & credit analysis” and “Smart CFO”. After signing in you request access inside the tool; TRUST approves the request and sets your role (upload, post or admin). We activate the tool within 24 working hours (Saturday–Thursday) of payment confirmation; it is activated automatically with the Professional or Credit & Institutions plan.

Limits of the results

AI extraction can misread figures or names, especially from unclear images. That is why nothing is posted automatically, and you are responsible for reviewing and approving the data and the entry. Tax treatment follows your company's settings and is not tax or legal advice.

Your data: processing and storage

Unlike Smart CFO, invoice files, their data and journal entries are stored on TRUST servers in private storage, visible only to your company's approved users. When you request extraction, the invoice image is sent to the TRUST AI service to read it. TRUST keeps your account, access status and the audit log.
